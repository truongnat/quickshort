ACTIONS LIVE ON THE THUMB — NOT ON A CARD
Replaces quick_access.rs. The toolbar already committed the action — the stack is a passive album, not a decision card. Spec: after the toolbar action, the captured region animates flying to the popup_pos corner and lands on the stack (~550ms spring). Newest on top, up to ~5 visible (older collapse into the stack, count chip on top). Hover → fans out; click thumb → reveal/open; thumb is a drag source into any app; right-click → actions; ◌ badge while uploading flips to ⧉ on success, ✕ on failure (click retries). Stack lingers until interacted; × on each thumb deletes. No countdown bar, no pills, no card.
