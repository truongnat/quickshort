✎

Annotate in place

Draw directly on the dimmed overlay — pen, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, highlighter, text, pixelate — everything confined to the captured region. The text tool is a real editing box: font sizes (12–48), bold toggle, caret, selection and ⌘A, with IME input for Vietnamese. Every shape and text block stays editable — the Select tool picks it up, drags it somewhere better, or deletes it. Strokes live on a physical-resolution layer, so they stay as sharp as the capture itself. Undo and redo are one click.

⌘

Global hotkey, resident tray

Quickshort sits in the menu bar. Press ⌘⇧S from anywhere and a dimmed overlay covers every monitor at once. Esc cancels, and the shortcut is re-recordable in Settings.

2×

Physical pixels, always

Captures request framebuffer resolution, not logical points. On a Retina display the PNG is 2880px wide and text stays crisp at 100% zoom. Mixed-DPI setups map each monitor through its own scale.

¶

OCR with OS engines

⌘T runs the selection through macOS Vision (Windows.Media.Ocr on Windows) and puts the text on your clipboard with a toast confirmation. Native engines, no bundled models, no cloud calls.

⇄

Cross-monitor selection

Start a drag on one display and finish it on another. Each monitor's crop is mapped through its own origin and DPI, then stitched into a single image.

⛓

Share links that outlive the app

⌘L uploads the capture to a Cloudflare edge worker and drops a public link on your clipboard — still serving the image 7 days later, long after you quit.

∅

Local-first, no account

No sign-up, no telemetry, nothing leaves your machine. Captures land in your folder, settings live in a plain config file, and sharing runs through a minimal relay — not third-party storage.

❯

Scriptable one-shot

quickshort --capture --out shot.png skips the tray lifecycle — select, act, exit. --delay waits for menus and tooltips, so it composes with launchd, CI jobs, or a shell alias.

↻

Self-updating

The tray checks GitHub Releases once a day. An update downloads the new .app, verifies its SHA-256, swaps it in place with rollback on failure, and relaunches. A stable self-signed identity keeps Screen Recording and Accessibility grants intact.

THE PIPELINE

Four layers. Zero browsers.

++
1

Capture

Every monitor lands in RAM in one shot. ScreenCaptureKit, GDI BitBlt, or PipeWire shm.

2

Overlay

Borderless window per monitor dims the frames and tracks the drag in virtual coordinates.

3

Map

Selection coordinates resolve through each monitor's origin and DPI scale factor.

4

Stitch

Per-monitor crops merge on a worker thread while the event loop stays free.
